# Neubrutalism

## Overview

Neubrutalism is a bold, playful evolution of classic web Brutalism that combines thick black outlines, flat saturated colors, hard offset shadows, and oversized typography into interfaces that feel energetic, confident, and intentionally unconventional. Emerging in the early 2020s through startups, indie products, and design communities, it transforms Brutalism's raw honesty into a friendlier aesthetic that embraces personality without sacrificing usability.

---

# Design Philosophy

Neubrutalism rejects polished corporate minimalism in favor of visual confidence and unmistakable personality. Rather than hiding interface elements behind subtle gradients and soft shadows, it celebrates their construction with bold borders, flat colors, and unapologetic geometry.

This style values:

- Honesty over polish
- Confidence over subtlety
- Personality over neutrality
- Function over decoration
- Simplicity over realism
- Playfulness over perfection

The interface should feel expressive, energetic, approachable, and memorable without becoming visually chaotic.

Users should immediately understand that the product has a unique point of view.

# Design DNA

Neubrutalism is immediately recognizable through thick outlines, geometric shadows, and vibrant flat colors.

### Visual Hierarchy

Hierarchy relies on:

- Bold typography
- Component size
- Layering
- Saturated color blocks

Large elements command attention immediately.

### Symmetry

Mostly structured.

Occasional playful asymmetry reinforces the handmade paper-cutout feeling.

### Shapes

Simple geometric forms dominate.

- Rectangles
- Rounded rectangles
- Circles
- Basic icons

Complex organic shapes are uncommon.

### Density

Medium.

Layouts remain spacious enough for large colorful elements to breathe.

### Ornamentation

Minimal.

Decoration comes from construction itself:

- Thick borders
- Hard shadows
- Flat color blocks
- Oversized icons
- Bold typography

### Contrast

Very high.

Black outlines create strong separation between every component.

### Materials

Inspired by:

- Screen printing
- Cut paper
- Comic books
- Posters
- Cardboard
- Plastic toys
- Sticker sheets

### Texture

Almost none.

Surfaces remain perfectly flat.

Visible texture is generally avoided.

### Lighting

Artificial.

Stylized.

Depth is created through graphic shadows rather than realistic lighting.

### Depth

Constructed entirely from hard offset shadows.

No blur.

No soft elevation.

Every layer feels like stacked paper.

# Visual Language

The interface should feel like a collection of colorful paper cutouts assembled by hand.

### Composition

Simple.

Bold.

Block-based.

Highly scannable.

### Balance

Balanced through geometry rather than subtle alignment.

### White Space

Moderate.

Enough spacing prevents saturated colors from becoming overwhelming.

### Rhythm

Created through repeated cards, borders, shadows, and bold typography.

### Scale

Large headlines.

Oversized buttons.

Prominent illustrations.

Bold CTAs.

### Repetition

Consistent repetition of:

- Thick outlines
- Flat colors
- Offset shadows
- Large corner radii (optional)
- Simple icons

### Patterns

Patterns emerge from modular layouts rather than decorative backgrounds.

### Decorative Elements

Decoration should remain simple:

- Smiley icons
- Abstract shapes
- Stars
- Arrows
- Stickers
- Geometric illustrations
- Hand-drawn accents (optional)

Avoid realism.

# Typography

Typography is loud, simple, and confident.

### Personality

Bold.

Friendly.

Confident.

Playful.

Direct.

### Classification

Heavy sans-serif typography dominates.

Rounded geometric sans-serifs work particularly well.

### Hierarchy

Large display headlines.

Heavy section titles.

Readable body copy.

Short UI labels.

Typography often functions as a graphic element.

### Weight

Heavy.

Bold and Extra Bold are used extensively.

Regular weights support body text.

### Letter Spacing

Neutral.

Slightly tighter display headlines.

Comfortable body spacing.

### Line Height

Display:

1.0–1.1

Body:

1.5–1.7

### Readability

Excellent.

Despite its bold appearance, readability remains high.

### Decorative Characteristics

Decoration comes from:

- Weight
- Scale
- Color
- Layout

Not from decorative typefaces.

### Modern Font Recommendations

- Inter
- Geist
- Space Grotesk
- Archivo Black
- IBM Plex Sans
- Plus Jakarta Sans
- General Sans
- Satoshi

# Color Direction

Neubrutalism embraces saturated, unapologetic colors.

### Primary Palette

- Bright yellow
- Bright red
- Bright blue
- Black
- White

### Accent Colors

Accent colors may include:

- Green
- Orange
- Purple
- Cyan
- Pink

Use only a few bold colors consistently.

### Neutral Colors

Primarily:

- White
- Black

Neutral grays are used sparingly.

### Contrast

Extremely high.

Every interactive element should stand out immediately.

### Materials

Colors should resemble:

- Screen-printed posters
- Plastic
- Children's toys
- Comic books
- Flat vector illustrations

### Surface Appearance

Completely flat.

No gradients.

No glass.

No glossy reflections.

### Light Mode

Preferred.

White backgrounds maximize color impact.

### Dark Mode

Equally effective.

Bright colors sit against solid black while preserving thick outlines.

# Layout Principles

Neubrutalism favors bold modular layouts over delicate composition.

### Grid Tendencies

Simple responsive grids.

Large blocks.

Clear alignment.

### Content Flow

Direct.

Fast.

Users move quickly from section to section.

### Alignment

Mostly strict.

Intentional overlaps create playful energy.

### Section Spacing

Generous.

Large colorful blocks should never feel cramped.

### Container Sizing

Wide layouts.

Large feature sections.

Prominent cards.

### Reading Order

Headline.

Supporting copy.

CTA.

Illustration.

Feature grid.

### Information Density

Medium.

Enough information to feel useful without overwhelming users.

# Motion

Motion should feel immediate and physical.

### Transition Style

Quick.

Snappy.

Confident.

### Hover Behavior

Small translation.

Shadow shifts.

Slight scaling.

Immediate color changes.

### Page Transitions

Minimal fades.

Small slides.

No cinematic motion.

### Scroll Behavior

Simple reveal animations.

Cards appear naturally.

Avoid excessive parallax.

### Microinteractions

Examples include:

- Shadow movement
- Button press animation
- Card lift
- Checkbox snap
- Toggle click

Motion should reinforce physicality.

### Loading States

Skeleton blocks.

Progress bars.

Graphic placeholders.

Avoid elaborate loaders.

# Best Use Cases

Neubrutalism excels for:

- Startup landing pages
- SaaS marketing
- Creative agencies
- Indie products
- AI startups
- Portfolio websites
- Design studios
- Creator tools
- No-code platforms
- Web3 products
- Community platforms
- Productivity apps
- Hackathon projects
- Developer tools
- Consumer apps targeting Gen Z

It performs well because it immediately differentiates products from the polished visual sameness common across modern SaaS.

---

# Avoid

Neubrutalism performs poorly for:

- Luxury brands
- Private banking
- Healthcare
- Government services
- Law firms
- Financial institutions
- High-end hospitality
- Classical museums
- Funeral services
- Medical software

These industries benefit from subtlety, refinement, and trust signals rather than visual boldness.

---

# Modern UI Translation

Neubrutalism should preserve its graphic boldness while adopting modern frontend best practices.

### Preserve

- Thick outlines
- Hard shadows
- Flat colors
- Bold typography
- Playful composition
- Graphic simplicity
- Paper-cut layering

### Modernize

- Responsive layouts
- Accessible contrast
- Component systems
- Variable fonts
- Responsive spacing
- Keyboard accessibility
- High-performance CSS transforms
- Dark mode support

### Avoid

- Soft gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Blurred shadows
- Excessive realism
- Thin borders
- Corporate neutrality
- Excessive decorative illustrations

### Evolution for Web Interfaces

Modern Neubrutalism should evolve into a mature design system capable of powering real digital products while preserving its rebellious visual identity. The style works best when expressive visuals are combined with excellent usability, accessibility, and responsive interaction rather than becoming purely aesthetic.
