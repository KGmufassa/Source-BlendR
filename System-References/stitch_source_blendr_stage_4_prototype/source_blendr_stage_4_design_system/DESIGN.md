# Source BlendR Stage 4 Design System

Precise, compact operations workspace for vendor import, discovery review, catalog curation, vendors, and AI provider settings.

Use warm off-white canvas, white/bordered surfaces, graphite text, muted secondary text, restrained amber primary actions, explicit status badges, visible focus rings, compact tables, accessible drawers, labeled forms, and no decorative hero/marketing treatment.

Every page must preserve the approved Stage 4 routes and workflows. Do not invent pages, features, roles, billing, checkout, onboarding, analytics dashboards, chat assistants, or admin/team management. All AI output remains a candidate until reviewed and explicitly imported.
